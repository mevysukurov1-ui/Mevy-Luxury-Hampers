Frequently Asked Questions

Can I provide photos, names, initials or a logo?

Yes, you may submit these. Suitability depends on product, materials, production method and file quality.

Do you accept celebrant and event orders?

Contact Mevy with the occasion, event date, quantity and gift ideas to discuss options and scheduling.

Can an order be covered by a contract?

For event, corporate or larger orders, scope and terms can be set out in a quotation, written order confirmation or contract, as agreed.

What about pricing, payment and delivery?

Final pricing, payment method, charges and delivery schedule are confirmed in the quotation based on products and destination.

How long do custom orders take?

Lead time depends on quantity, personalisation, materials and availability. Share your event date early.
